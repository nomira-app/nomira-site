How the bouncer works and safety

Effective October 17, 2026

If you're in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (US Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) any time, or call your local emergency number. Outside the US, find a helpline at findahelpline.com.

The bouncer is an AI

When you argue your case at the Door, you're talking to an AI, not a person. It reads your excuse and suggests whether to let you in, ask you to earn the time, or say no. It can be funny or blunt, but it roasts the excuse, never you: it's told never to comment on your looks, identity, family or intelligence.

The AI only makes a suggestion. Nomira's server makes the final call using fixed limits (for example, a few short unlocks a day) that the AI can't change, so tricking it can't get you more time than your plan allows.

The AI is Anthropic's Claude. It only reads your excuses if you allow it on the screen that asks first, and you can turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy. With it off, the Door still works: you can walk for time or use your emergency pass. Nomira is for ages 13 and up, but the AI is for adults aged 18 and older only. For ages 13 to 17 the AI stays off, and our server enforces it.

What happens if you mention a crisis

  1. If your message mentions self-harm, suicide, abuse or being in danger, the bouncer drops the act and answers kindly. If the AI can't be reached, the app still checks for clear signs of danger.
  2. The app shows the 988 Lifeline, with a button to call it.
  3. The Door never treats that message as an excuse, and it never counts toward your stats or streak.
  4. In your history, it shows as "Support offered", never as the words you typed.

Nomira isn't a crisis service, a therapist or a medical product, and it can't contact anyone for you. It's a tool to help you cut back on screen time.

Built to be put down

Nomira is designed to get you off your phone, not to keep you chatting. Each try at the Door allows one excuse, and the AI can be asked a limited number of times a day. It doesn't offer companionship features.
