Nomira

Support

Email hello@nomira.app and we'll get back to you, usually within two days.

Cancel or change your subscription

Apple bills Nomira. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Nomira. Cancel at least a day before your trial or period ends to avoid being charged. Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel it.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore a purchase on a new phone

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Nomira, and tap Restore on the paywall or in Settings.

Erase your data

Settings → Privacy → Erase my Door history erases everything you've typed. Settings → Delete account deletes your whole account. See the Privacy Policy.

Turn the AI bouncer on or off

Settings → Privacy → Let the AI read my excuses. With it off, nothing you type is sent to the AI, and you can still walk for time or use your emergency pass.

Safety

If you're in danger, call or text 988 (US) or your local emergency number. Read how the bouncer works and safety.
